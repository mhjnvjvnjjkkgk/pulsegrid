## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium, high-urgency emergency navigation environment tailored for critical, split-second decision making. The experience merges the precision of aerospace instrumentation with the optical depth of Apple-inspired liquid glassmorphism. It projects absolute authority, clinical reliability, and rapid computational clarity without sacrificing sensory refinement.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Engineered for patients, paramedics, and caregivers facing time-sensitive medical emergencies. The interface instills calm, collected focus under stress through an obsidian abyss backdrop, piercing vital sign indicators, and tactile optical refraction. The visual atmosphere evokes high-end medical telemetry, calm assurance, and seamless kinetic guidance.

### Design Direction: Liquid Obsidian Glassmorphism
The aesthetic combines multi-layered translucent glass panels, luminous fluid dynamics, and specular border highlights floating over deep atmospheric slate voids. Volumetric glows and refracted glass orbs serve as real-time triage beacons, creating tangible spatial hierarchy and immediate focus on vital emergency metrics (e.g., live ICU bed availability, ER wait times, transit telemetry).

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an abyss-deep obsidian slate foundation, punctuated by high-frequency luminescent neon accents engineered to maximize contrast, visual triage, and legible signal-to-noise ratios in dark or chaotic conditions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#00F0FF` - Neon Cyan):** Represents rapid transit, digital clarity, navigation pathways, and live network telemetry. Used for active glass borders, directional vectors, focal interactive buttons, and primary data highlights.
- **Secondary (`#00FF87` - Emerald Pulse):** Denotes medical verification, open capacity, immediate ER readiness, verified survival vitals, and normal wait status.
- **Tertiary (`#FF3366` - Hyper Red):** Dedicated exclusively to critical triage levels, severe trauma alerts, critical delays, and emergency broadcast triggers.
- **Neutral Base (`#05070B` - Deep Obsidian Slate):** An ink-black void infused with slight cool-slate undertones, serving as the canvas for fluid lighting effects and translucent frosted glass plates.

### Atmospheric Glass Surfaces
- **Glass Base Plate:** `rgba(13, 19, 33, 0.65)` with backdrop blur.
- **Elevated Glass Card:** `rgba(20, 29, 48, 0.45)` with ambient cyan inner specular reflection.
- **Frosted Overlay:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` over deep slate.
- **Border Specular Highlight:** Linear gradient from `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.45)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` at 45 degrees.

## Typography

The typography system unites structural computational forms with surgical reading performance:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers aerodynamic, progressive authority. Geometric contours cut cleanly across translucent interfaces, anchoring hospital names, emergency triage titles, and rapid destination cards.
- **Body (`Geist`):** Provides clinical, ultra-neutral legibility. Its clean vertical metrics and distinct glyph contours ensure instantaneous legibility even under glare, motion, or severe distress.
- **Labels & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Handles wait timers, ETA millisecond tracking, bed capacities, triage codes, and GPS distance markers. Monospaced tabular figures avoid layout shifting during high-frequency live data updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around an ergonomic thumb-zone mobile hierarchy that prioritizes one-handed activation during movement.

### Fluid Rhythm & Spacing Principles
- **Grid Structure:** Fluid 4-column layout on mobile, transitioning to a dynamic 8-column layout on tablet/foldable displays with standard 16px gutters and minimum 20px edge safety margins.
- **Bottom-Heavy Ergonomics:** The bottom 45% of the viewport is reserved for interactive cards, triage switches, and one-tap emergency calls, keeping interactive triggers away from awkward stretch zones.
- **Spatial Stacking:** Vertical space follows a continuous 8pt rhythm, reserving tighter 4px gaps for nested telemetry badges and 24px/32px clusters for discrete hospital information modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is rendered not through artificial drop shadows, but through refractive optical depth, frosted density, and chromatic ambient glow fields.

### Layer Architecture
1. **The Slate Abyss (Base Canvas, Level 0):** Flat `#05070B` deep obsidian background featuring ambient fluid gradient orbs (blurred at 80px to 120px) softly drifting to indicate systemic urgency or status.
2. **Sub-Surface Frosted Sheet (Level 1):** `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`; surface fill `rgba(10, 16, 26, 0.65)` with a 1px perimeter outline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
3. **Floating Glass Cockpit (Level 2):** Critical floating sheets (bottom triage drawer, live navigation card) use `backdrop-filter: blur(40px) saturate(200%)`, surface `rgba(18, 26, 43, 0.55)`, and a top-to-bottom edge highlight gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.5), rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05) 50%, rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.2))`.
4. **Active Luminescent Elements (Level 3):** Urgent actions, primary dispatch rings, and live pulse dots emit localized neon corona glows: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4), inset 0 0 12px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined modern curvature (`roundedness: 2`, where base elements receive 0.5rem / 8px, cards receive 1rem / 16px, and primary panels receive 1.5rem / 24px).

### Optical Shaping Characteristics
- **Dynamic Surface Radii:** Glass sheets and bottom modal containers feature continuous squircle curves (continuous curvature mimicking native iOS liquid glass).
- **Control Pills:** Emergency status pills, active filter chips, and interactive thumb selectors maintain fully rounded pill terminations (`9999px`) to contrast structurally against tabular glass grid cards.
- **Fluid Cutaways:** Secondary telemetry data points sit within softly recessed nested containers with 8px inner radii, ensuring structural nesting balance within the parent 16px card.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Emergency Triggers
- **Primary Dispatch Action:** Pill-shaped, high-intensity neon cyan background (`#00F0FF`) with jet obsidian bold text (`#05070B`), featuring an internal rim highlight and ambient `0 0 32px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.45)` glow.
- **SOS Critical Beacon Button:** Hyper-red glass (`rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.15)`) enclosed by a pulsing specular border (`rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.7)`), displaying high-contrast monospaced alert indicators with tactile spring press feedback.
- **Glass Auxiliary Button:** Translucent backdrop blur (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), hairline 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), active state illuminating with emerald pulse highlights.

### 2. Live Triage & Metric Chips
- Pill contours housing dual-state indicators: a micro-luminous pulsing glass sphere (6px) accompanied by monospaced tabular metrics (`JetBrains Mono`).
- **Open / Rapid State:** Emerald neon tint (`rgba(0, 255, 135, 0.12)`) with `#00FF87` typography and border.
- **Congested / Delayed State:** Hyper-red tint (`rgba(255, 51, 102, 0.12)`) with `#FF3366` typography.

### 3. Hospital Telemetry Cards
- Double-layered glass morphism: translucent obsidian container accented with a 45-degree light sweep along the outer border.
- Top section holds hospital title (`Space Grotesk`) and distance matrix; middle segment hosts dynamic capacity bars (visualizing ICU and trauma bays); bottom row anchors instant one-tap navigation and emergency call conduits.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Glass trench styling: background `rgba(5, 7, 11, 0.75)` recessed inside an elevated glass panel with an inset subtle shadow.
- Cyan focus indicator: upon focus, the hairline border shifts from `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` to `#00F0FF`, accompanied by a subtle cyan ambient aura.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Outer circular glass rings with a 1.5px border. On selection, an emerald or cyan glowing orb scales into view from center, casting an internal radial illumination.

### 6. Emergency Floating Radar HUD (Specialized Component)
- A persistent floating glass pill docked at the bottom navigation boundary that continuously projects proximity to the nearest verified trauma center, dynamic live minutes ETA, and direct rapid rerouting triggers.
